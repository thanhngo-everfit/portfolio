About

I'm Thanh — a product manager who came up through Scrum, so I care as much about how a team ships as what it ships.

I work on the Core Product team at Everfit, a B2B platform that helps fitness coaches run their business and train their clients. My days move between coach and client problems, the people building the solution, and the data that tells us whether it worked.

I also lead a team of Business Analysts — coaching them on writing clear specs, asking better questions and growing into product roles.

How I work

/01

Start from the problem

Before any solution, get clear on who hurts, how often, and what "better" looks like.

/02

Write it down

A clear spec is a kindness to engineers, designers and future-me.

/03

Ship in slices

Small, safe releases beat big reveals — learn early, adjust early.

/04

Close the loop

After launch, look at the data and talk to users. Done means it worked.
